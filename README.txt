VYBEX — informational catalog build

This build intentionally does NOT implement:
- online ordering or checkout
- BLIK/payment collection
- delivery ordering
- referral/free-product promotion
- automated distance sales

Reason: Polish law prohibits distance sales of electronic cigarettes and refill containers, and regulates advertising/promotion. Verify the current legal status with a Polish lawyer/compliance specialist before enabling any commercial workflow.

The supplied product quantities are treated as stock figures and are not displayed to customers.
Prices are intentionally omitted from this compliance-safe build.
